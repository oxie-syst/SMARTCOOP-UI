Privacy Policy

SmartCoop — Smart Poultry Management

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, SmartCoop may process your name, email address, phone number, account details, farm profile, coop plans, poultry records, and information you provide to AI-assisted features. If you use Google Sign-In, we may receive basic account information needed for authentication.

2. How Information Is Used

We use information to create and manage accounts, provide poultry planning tools, save farm records, display recommendations, support users, and maintain the operation and security of SmartCoop. Newsletter emails should be sent only to people who have subscribed or otherwise provided appropriate permission.

3. Storage and Service Providers

SmartCoop may use hosting, database, authentication, email, and AI service providers to deliver its features. Information sent to these providers depends on the feature used and their respective processing arrangements.

4. AI-Assisted Features

Information you submit to AI tools may be processed to generate responses or recommendations. Avoid entering passwords, financial account details, or other sensitive personal information into AI prompts.

5. Data Protection

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

Information should be kept only as long as necessary for legitimate service purposes or applicable legal obligations. Specific retention periods and account deletion procedures should be confirmed by the SmartCoop team before this policy is published.

7. Your Privacy Choices

You may request information about your personal data and ask about correction or deletion through SmartCoop's official contact channel, subject to applicable requirements. Newsletter subscribers should be provided a way to unsubscribe.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

SmartCoop may use browser storage and similar technologies to support sign-in, preferences, and application functionality. The exact technologies used should be reviewed and disclosed accurately.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy as features and data practices change. The updated version will show a revised date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the official SmartCoop contact channel listed on the website.
